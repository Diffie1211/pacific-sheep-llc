About the firm

An accountant who knows small-business books from the inside

Pacific Sheep LLC was founded by Diffie Liu, a financial analyst who holds a Master of Accountancy from Wake Forest University and is a CPA candidate who has passed three of the four CPA exam sections. Her work centers on small businesses across many industries — the chart-of-accounts redesigns, per-unit cost tracking, and month-end discipline that growing companies’ books demand.

That means your books aren’t handled by someone guessing at how your business works. Sales, service revenue, payroll, leases, inventory — they each land in the right place, and your monthly report explains what changed and why it matters.

The firm is deliberately small. You work directly with the person doing your books, every month.

FAQ

Common questions

What kinds of businesses do you work with?+

Small businesses of all kinds — service companies, retailers, restaurants, contractors, logistics operations, and more. If your books have moving parts that a generic bookkeeper might miss, that’s exactly the work we do. Email us and we’ll tell you honestly whether we’re the right fit.

Monthly fee, hourly, or flat fee — which should I choose?+

Ongoing bookkeeping is a monthly fee: Light $300–$500/month (up to 100 transactions, 1 bank account + 1 card, no payroll or inventory), Standard $600–$1,000/month (up to 300 transactions, multiple accounts, bank / credit card / loan reconciliations, monthly P&L and balance sheet), or Complex $1,200–$2,500/month (high volume, multiple business lines or locations, or 1099s, payroll coordination, inventory, or vehicles & equipment). Consulting is $75 per hour. One-time projects are a flat fee or $75/hour. We’ll recommend whichever fits your situation after a short review of your books.

My books are months behind. Can you catch them up?+

Yes — catch-up and cleanup work is a common starting point. Cleanup of past books is priced separately and is not included in the monthly bookkeeping fee. We’ll review what exists, give you a separate quote for bringing everything current, and then move you onto a normal monthly rhythm.

I’m thinking of selling my business. What does Exit-Ready Financials cover?+

It’s the financial preparation before a sale — for owners considering any buyer, including private equity. We clean up and organize your books, prepare financial statements that can withstand buyer due diligence, and assemble the support schedules used in valuation. The engagement is priced as a flat fee by project, quoted in writing before work begins. We handle the accounting side only: we do not negotiate transactions, search for buyers, or manage closings.

Are you a CPA firm?+

Pacific Sheep LLC provides bookkeeping, reporting, and accounting consulting. Founder Diffie Liu is a CPA candidate who has passed three of the four CPA exam sections; the firm does not hold itself out as a licensed CPA firm and does not perform audits or attest work. For tax filing, we prepare clean, organized year-end packages that make your tax preparer’s job faster and cheaper.

How do we communicate?+

By email at pacificsheepinfo@gmail.com. It keeps a written record of every request and answer — which, for accounting, is exactly what you want.
